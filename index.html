3. Seven Golden Rules of Stroke Order

1
Top to Bottom: Draw upper parts before lower ones (三 • 早 • 言).
2
Left to Right: Draw the left side before the right side (你 • 明 • 吃).
3
Horizontal before Vertical: Cross horizontal bars first (十 • 土 • 木).
4
Left-Falling before Right-Falling: Left slash first (人 • 八 • 大).
5
Outside before Inside: Frame the exterior first (月 • 同 • 问).
6
Close Boxes Last: Fill the inside before sealing the bottom (日 • 四 • 国).
7
Center before Sides: Anchor the center axis first (小 • 水 • 木).

1. Four Core Character Structures

Radical Layouts

Every Mandarin character arranges its building blocks into one of four clean geometric templates inside the Mi Zi Ge square:

Left-Right 你好

Place the semantic side root (亻 or 女) on the left and the phonetic piece on the right (亻 + 尔 = 你).

Top-Bottom 早上

Stack pieces along the center vertical line with upper roots above lower bases (日 + 十 = 早, 艹 + 人 + 木 = 茶).

Enclosure 问国

Drop the outer frame (门, 囗) centered on the grid, then place the inner root cleanly inside the pocket (门 + 口 = 问).

Half-Enclosure 远店

Place open corner frames (辶, 广, 厂) first, then tuck the interior piece into the open space (辶 + 元 = 远).
