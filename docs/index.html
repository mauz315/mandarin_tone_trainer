3. The 7 Golden Rules of Stroke Order (笔顺规则)

Core Principles
1
Top to Bottom (从上到下): Draw higher components before lower ones. 三 (top → mid → bottom) • 早 • 言
2
Left to Right (从左到右): Write the left radical before the right side. 你 (亻 then 尔) • 明 (日 then 月) • 吃
3
Horizontal before Vertical (先横后竖): Lay the horizontal foundation before the pillar. 十 (一 then 丨) • 土 • 木
4
Left-Falling before Right-Falling (先撇后捺): Slashes falling left are drawn first. 人 (丿 then ㇏) • 八 • 天 • 大
5
Outside before Inside (先外后内): Frame the outer structure before filling the interior. 月 (outer frame then inner bars) • 同 • 风
6
"Enter the Room, Then Close the Door" (先进入后关门): For closed boxes, seal the bottom bar last! 日 (丨, then 𠃍, then inner 一, seal with 一) • 四 • 国
7
Center before Sides (先中间后两边): Plant the symmetrical center first, then wings. 小 (center 亅 then left/right dots) • 水 • 木
lightbulb Learner Pro-Tips
  • Don't stress tiny hooks: In testing mode, drawing a straight line (丨) is accepted for terminal hooks (亅).
  • One box = three strokes: The mouth radical 口 is NOT a circular loop. Draw left vertical, top-right turn, and seal the bottom.
  • Downward-falling slashes: Slashes (撇 & 捺) always start at the top and fall outward. Never draw them upward from the bottom!
